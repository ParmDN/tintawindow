### Diagnosis reported before editing

Read check-content.py and keyword_matching.py before making the edit. Content.handle_data collects text nodes inside main, including headings, spans and closed FAQ answers, but excludes nav, script, style, SVG and hidden/aria-hidden content. It does not collect attribute values, image alt text or metadata. The popup dialog is outside main and is not counted. Photographic captions are counted when they are ordinary text within main.

The natural matcher lowercases, strips combining accents, replaces hyphens with spaces, converts & to and, and collapses whitespace. For a keyword ending in Christchurch it permits an optional in, across or for before the location and requires word boundaries. Consequently window film in Christchurch, window film across Christchurch, window film for Christchurch and window film Christchurch-wide each count once; Christchurch Window Films does not match.
