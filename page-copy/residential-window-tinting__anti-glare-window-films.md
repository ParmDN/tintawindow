## Questions about Anti-glare window film

### Can film remove every screen reflection? +

No. Film can reduce glare from sunlight through the treated glass, but lamps, other windows and screen position may still create reflections. Tell us what you see from your normal working or viewing position.

### Will the room feel too dark? +

Different products reduce different amounts of light. We help you compare suitable samples so the result suits both comfort and the daylight you want.

### Is it useful for a home office? +

Yes. We fit Anti-glare window film in Christchurch for suitable home-office glass as well as living spaces. Send a photograph showing the window, desk and screen to start the discussion.

### Can glare film help with heat too? +

Some products reduce both solar heat and brightness. A suitable solar film may also ease demand on cooling, but glare reduction alone does not establish an energy saving. We compare the selected film’s performance for your glass.

### Do all the windows need the same film? +

Not necessarily. Affected windows can be assessed together, with appearance considered across the room. Different exposure or glass may call for different products.
