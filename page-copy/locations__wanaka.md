Decisions worth making early

## What to check before choosing film.

### Daylight and the outlook

Say which views and clear areas matter most. Compare a suitable sample from inside and outside so heat or glare control is balanced with the look you want.

### Guest privacy after dark

Do not rely on reflective film for privacy with lights on inside at night. Frosted coverage or other window coverings may be needed. Explain how bedrooms and bathrooms are used.

### Access between stays

Share bookings, a local access contact and any limits on room use. We agree the work and preparation first, rather than assume every gap between guests is suitable for fitting.

## Questions for your Wānaka project.

### Can I enquire while I am away? +

Yes. Send photos, the location and the person who can provide access. We can discuss the first steps and what needs to be checked before a product or visit is agreed.

### Do guests need care instructions? +

Keep the chosen film’s cleaning guidance with the property instructions. Staff and guests should know how to care for the treated panes and who to contact about a concern.

### What if room use changes later? +

Tell us what has changed, such as a desk, bed or seating position. The new concern may involve a different window or view, even if the original film is still doing its job.

After you choose

## Leave clear care instructions for the next stay

For a Wānaka holiday home or guest property, keep the film care guidance where the person managing the property can find it. Confirm the advised time before cleaning and pass the instructions to anyone who looks after the windows. Care should follow the selected product, not a general rule.

Record which panes were treated and which were left clear. If guests raise a privacy or glare concern later, ask which room and time of day were involved. That detail helps you decide whether the concern relates to the existing film or a different use of the space that needs fresh advice.
