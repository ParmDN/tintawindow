## Questions about commercial frosted, opaque and decorative film

### Does frosted film provide sound privacy? +

No. Frosting obscures views; it does not soundproof a meeting room. If spoken privacy matters, that needs a separate assessment of the room and its construction.

### Can the finish include our logo? +

Suitable decorative treatments can be discussed around your artwork and glass. We confirm the proposed layout and requirements before fitting rather than assuming every design will suit every surface.

### Will it block all daylight? +

Frosting admits soft, scattered light. Opaque and blackout products have different light transmission, so check the actual product. The area covered also changes how the room feels.

### Can some of the glass stay clear? +

Yes, full and partial coverage can be discussed. Consider seated and standing views, adjoining panes and the appearance from both sides when agreeing the layout.

### Can you fit around office hours? +

We discuss access, room availability and the person who can approve the work. Timing and disruption are agreed for the building before installation.
