## Window tinting in Christchurch: prepare for your assessment

A site visit should answer the questions a photograph cannot. Put together a window list with room names, approximate sizes and the main concern in each space. Mark any panes replaced at a different time. That helps keep the assessment focused on the glass included in your enquiry.

Decide who will view the samples and approve the work. If you want less heat in one room and privacy in another, record those separately. Bring your preferred appearance, questions and any building information to the same conversation so the resulting quote is easier to understand.

Decisions worth making early

## What to check before choosing film.

### Different rooms, different needs

A study may need less glare while a bathroom needs an obscured view. List the rooms and when each problem occurs. We can discuss them together without assuming one film suits every window.

### Glass worth checking first

Share what you know about the panes, coatings and any old film. A photo of a label can help. Similar-looking windows may contain different glass, so each proposed film needs a suitability check.

### A clear next step

Tell us which windows to include and who can provide access. We will discuss whether more details or a visit are needed before you compare samples and agree to the work.

Advice, installation & aftercare

## What helps us plan your visit

### Room and pane list

Group the pane list by room and mark what you want the visit to establish. Glass suitability, appearance and price are different questions.

### Sample decisions

Name the person who can compare samples and approve the work. If an owner is absent, explain how their decision will be obtained.

### A proposal you can follow

Check that the proposed product and treated panes are identified. Ask about any detail that is unclear before agreeing to the work.

### Care kept with the project

Keep the film name, installation information and cleaning guidance together. They are useful if another person manages or maintains the property later.

How It Works

## Arrange an assessment and understand the next step

### List the glass to assess

Give each room a name and identify the panes you want assessed. Add the main question for each: suitability, privacy, heat or appearance.

### Agree who provides access

Confirm who can provide access and discuss any room in uses. Share known glass labels, coatings or old film before the visit.

### Review samples in context

Compare suitable samples from inside and outside. Keep the room’s priorities beside the product information so the decision has a clear purpose.

### Confirm the written proposal

Check that the proposal identifies the product and windows included. Ask which details still need confirmation before accepting the work.

### Prepare for fitting and care

Agree preparation and installation arrangements. Keep the selected film’s care and warranty information with the assessment and quote records.

## Questions for your Christchurch project.

### Can you look at several concerns together? +

Yes. Send a room list with the main issue in each space. We can discuss heat, glare, fading and privacy together, then explain where different films may make sense.

### What if only one window needs help? +

Tell us about that pane and the change you want. Include nearby windows in a photograph so we can consider how a finish would look alongside glass left untreated.

### How do I arrange an assessment? +

Share your location, photographs and contact details. We will confirm what needs checking and discuss access and timing for your property. You do not have to choose a film before asking.

After you choose

## Keep your quote easy to compare

Once you have discussed the rooms in your Christchurch property, check that the quote names the selected film and the panes to be treated. If different products are proposed, ask which concern each one addresses. This makes it easier to compare the options on the same basis.

Keep samples, window photographs and care information together. If you decide to improve another room later, these records help explain the earlier choice. Tell us about any new glass or changes to the room, as the next window may need a different product even when you want a similar appearance.
