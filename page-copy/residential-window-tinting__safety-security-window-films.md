Door glass and sidelights may need assessment where impact or damage is a concern. Film suitability and fitting details matter alongside the pane itself.

### Worry about broken glass spreading

A correctly selected film can help retain fragments if glass breaks. The result depends on the glass, film, installation and the type of incident.

### Concern about forced entry

Explain the security problem, not just a preferred film thickness. Any claim about delaying entry needs evidence for the proposed glass, film and attachment system.

Some safety films are clear-looking. We discuss appearance alongside the protection required, rather than assuming safety film has to be dark or reflective.

If the concern is people seeing in, privacy or frosting may be the right starting point. Looking private does not prove a pane has safety or security performance.

Common Questions

## Questions about Safety & security window film

### Does film make glass unbreakable? +

No. Suitable film can help hold broken fragments together, but the glass can still break. We discuss the intended role and the limits of the particular glass and film system.

### Can it stop every break-in? +

No system should be presented as a guarantee against every break-in. Any proposed resistance to forced entry needs relevant evidence for the installed glass, frame, film and attachment method.

### Will it change the appearance? +

Some products are clear-looking; others have a visible finish. We can discuss Safety & security window film in Christchurch with appearance and suitable protection considered together.

### Is film a way to avoid replacing unsafe glass? +

Do not assume that it is. The existing glass and the application must be assessed. Where a particular glass safety standard or compliance requirement applies, the proposed solution needs appropriate confirmation.

### What should I send you? +

Send photographs of the pane and frame, its position in the home and any information you have about the glass. Describe whether you are concerned about accidental breakage, loose fragments or security.
