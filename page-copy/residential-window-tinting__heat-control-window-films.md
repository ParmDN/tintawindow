## Questions about Heat control window film

### Will film cool the room by a set number of degrees? +

We do not promise a fixed temperature drop. Film can reduce solar heat through suitable glass, but room temperature also depends on ventilation, insulation, cooling and how the building is used.

### Will I lose the view? +

Many solar films preserve a view out, with different levels of colour and reflection. Compare suitable samples rather than choosing by shade alone. Frosting has a different purpose and obscures the outlook.

### Can you help with just one hot room? +

Yes. Heat control window film in Christchurch can be considered for a single room or selected windows. Tell us the number of panes, approximate sizes and the time of day the heat is worst.

### Can it go on double glazing? +

The film must suit the actual glass and any coatings. We discuss that before product selection. A film used successfully on one property is not automatically suitable for another.

### Can window film help lower my power bill? +

A suitable solar film may reduce the electricity used for air conditioning or a heat pump in cooling mode. Savings depend on the film, glass, window direction, local climate and how cooling is used. Solar film also reduces useful winter sun, so whole-year savings are not guaranteed. We help you weigh summer comfort against colder-season needs.
