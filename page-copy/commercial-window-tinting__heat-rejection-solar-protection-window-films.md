## Questions about Commercial heat control window film

### Will film reduce the temperature by a fixed amount? +

No fixed temperature change is promised. The effect depends on the film, glass, exposure and the rest of the building, including cooling, ventilation and how the space is used.

### Does solar protection have to look dark? +

No. We compare Solar protection window films in Christchurch with different appearances and performance. Ask to see suitable samples and product information instead of selecting on colour alone.

### Can film reduce our electricity bills? +

Suitable solar film may lower electricity use for cooling by reducing incoming solar heat. Savings depend on the film, glass, window direction, climate and the building’s operation. Reduced winter solar warmth can affect heating needs too. Assess the whole building and year, rather than assuming a product’s heat-rejection figure equals a percentage off the bill.

### Can it be fitted to coated or double-glazed panes? +

Compatibility must be checked for the actual glass and coating. Include any glass information you have, and do not assume that film suited to another building is suitable for yours.

### How do you plan a commercial installation? +

We discuss the affected areas, access, working hours and who can authorise the work. Product and fitting arrangements are agreed before installation dates are confirmed.
