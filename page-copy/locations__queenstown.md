Decisions worth making early

## What to check before choosing film.

### The view from your usual seat

Show the window from the sofa, desk or dining table where sunlight bothers you. A film needs to suit the people using that spot, not just look good on a small sample.

### Interiors exposed to sunlight

Point out carpets, timber flooring, furniture or artwork at risk of fading. We can compare UV, light and heat performance together. Film can help reduce sunlight exposure, but cannot stop every cause of fading.

### A plan for samples and access

Agree who can view samples and approve the product if the owner is away. Include bookings and preparation needs early so visit and fitting dates can be discussed clearly.

## Questions for your Queenstown project.

### Do I have to choose a dark film? +

No. Compare suitable products for heat, light and reflection rather than colour alone. A sample helps you judge the change in your room and from outside.

### Can you help with a guest property? +

Tell us about the rooms, guest use and access contact. We can discuss suitable film and a practical fitting plan. Travel, timing and availability are confirmed for the job.

### Can photographs replace an assessment? +

Photos help start the discussion but cannot show every detail of the glass. We will explain what else needs checking before recommending a film for the panes.

After you choose

## Keep the owner and property manager informed

For a Queenstown property managed on your behalf, share the agreed film choice, treated windows and care instructions with the person looking after it. They can pass the right cleaning guidance to the team and help identify a window if you have a question later.
