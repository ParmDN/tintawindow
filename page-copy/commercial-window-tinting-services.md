## Window problems that affect your working day

### Hot desks and reception areas

Suitable solar film can reduce heat entering through the glass. In air-conditioned spaces, that may reduce cooling demand and associated electricity costs. The result depends on the film, glass and building use.

### Screens competing with sunlight

Ease bright light on monitors, meeting-room screens and classroom boards. We consider where people sit as well as the window itself.

### Meetings visible through partitions

Create privacy from view in meeting rooms or staff areas. Frosted and decorative coverage can define those spaces while keeping a lighter feel than a solid partition.

### Shopfronts exposed to damage

Scratched or etched glass affects presentation and maintenance. A suitable replaceable protective film can help protect the surface; it cannot repair existing damage.

### Glass safety and security concerns

Assess the intended protection before choosing a product. Safety film, security systems and anti-graffiti films have different roles and should not be treated as interchangeable.

### Work that needs to fit your day

Tell us about opening hours, customers, tenants and access. Clear planning helps us discuss installation arrangements without promising that every site can be treated without disruption.

## Frequently asked questions about commercial window film

### Can film help without replacing the windows? +

Film can improve suitable existing glass for particular needs. The glass, coatings, condition and exposure must be considered first. It is not a substitute for every type of window upgrade.

### Can you work around our opening hours? +

We discuss access, timing and the areas that need to remain available. Arrangements are agreed for the site; after-hours or uninterrupted work should not be assumed before that discussion.

### Can one product handle all our needs? +

Sometimes a product addresses more than one concern, but heat, glare, privacy and protection have different requirements. We help you compare the priorities instead of assuming one film suits every pane.

### Do you work on rented premises? +

We can discuss Commercial window tinting in Christchurch for tenants and property owners. Confirm who can approve the work and any change in external appearance before installation is agreed.

### What information helps with a quote? +

Send the location, photographs, approximate pane sizes and main problem. Include access restrictions, opening hours and the person who can approve the selected film.
