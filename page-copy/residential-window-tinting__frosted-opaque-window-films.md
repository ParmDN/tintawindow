Common Questions

## Questions about Frosted and opaque window film

### Does frosted film work at night? +

It obscures a clear view without relying on daytime light balance. The selected finish, lighting, coverage and how close someone stands to the glass still matter, so compare a sample for the privacy you need.

### Does opaque film block all daylight? +

Products vary. For opaque film, we compare the selected finish rather than assuming every product is blackout. Frosted film admits soft, scattered light while obscuring the view.

### Can I keep part of the glass clear? +

Yes, coverage can be discussed for the room and window layout. Tell us where someone can see in while standing or sitting, and which parts of the outlook you want to keep.

### Can it be used on a front door? +

Suitable film can be considered for door panes and sidelights, subject to the glass and application. Include the full entrance in your photograph so the finish can be planned as a whole.

### Is frosting the same as safety film? +

No. Frosting is selected for privacy and appearance. Safety needs require separate consideration of the glass, the product and the installation.
