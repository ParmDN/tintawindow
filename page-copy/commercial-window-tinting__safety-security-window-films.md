Discuss help holding broken pieces together. The selected film and fitting need to suit the pane and its use.

Ask whether the film needs a fixing at the frame. Keep that detail with the proposed product and test information.

### Check what is already there

Include known coatings, glass type and damage. Film does not remove every need to replace unsafe glass or carry out other work.

Raise privacy as a separate need. A finish that hides the view does not prove how a pane behaves if hit.

## Questions about Commercial safety & security window film

### Does film make commercial glass unbreakable? +

No. Glass can still break. Suitable film can help hold pieces together, with the result depending on the glass, film, fitting and incident.

### Will it stop every break-in? +

No such guarantee should be assumed. Ask which test data supports the glass, frame, film and any fixings proposed for your building.

### Can you discuss a written building requirement? +

Yes. We discuss Commercial safety & security window film in Christchurch for the concerns you describe. The proposed product and work must be checked for that use.

### Is this a replacement for anti-graffiti film? +

No. Anti-graffiti film protects a surface from suitable types of marking. Safety and security films have different jobs and need their own checks.

### What should I send first? +

Send photos of the panes and frames, known glass details and the written need. Name who approves the work and who can provide access.
