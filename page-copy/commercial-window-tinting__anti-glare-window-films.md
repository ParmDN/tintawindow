## Questions about Commercial anti-glare window film

### Can film remove every reflection? +

No. It can reduce sunlight through the treated glass, but lighting, other windows and screen position may still create reflections. Show us what the user sees so those factors can be considered.

### Is this suitable for schools and classrooms? +

Commercial anti-glare window films in Christchurch can be considered for suitable educational glass. Tell us about boards, screens, seating and access, and we will discuss the appropriate assessment.

### Will the workplace look too dark? +

Products offer different levels of light reduction. Compare a suitable sample in the space, considering the people who use it and the daylight they need for their work.

### Can one film help with heat as well? +

Some films combine glare and solar heat control. Where cooling is used, less incoming heat may also reduce electricity demand. That depends on the selected film, the building and its use; a glare rating alone does not establish savings.

### Can you install while we are open? +

We discuss your operation, access and the work area before confirming arrangements. Practical scheduling is agreed for the site, with any disruption explained.
