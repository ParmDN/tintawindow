Christchurch Safety Window Films

Plan glass protection around the risk.

Busy doors and exposed panes can raise concerns about broken glass. We discuss Commercial safety & security window film in Christchurch for shops, offices and shared spaces.

Suitable safety film can help hold broken glass together. Glass can still break. For a security need, ask what test data supports the glass, frame and film as fitted. We explain what needs checking before you rely on it.

The problem, and a better way to use the space

Glass protection for busy buildings

Loose glass after a break

Suitable film can help hold pieces together when a pane breaks. The film and glass need checking for that job.

A shopfront at risk of forced entry

Tell us what risk you need to address. A security claim needs test data for the glass, frame, film and any fixings.

Doors beside busy routes

Show where people pass close to the glass. Include the frame and any known damage when you ask about the pane.

A building with written rules

Share the written needs from the owner or property team. The proposed work must be checked against what the building requires.

A clear finish you want to keep

Some films look clear. Compare the look with the job the film needs to do; colour alone does not prove safety.

Scratches rather than broken glass

Ask about anti-graffiti film for surface marks. That is a different job from holding broken pieces or resisting forced entry.

Assess the complete protection system

Discuss Commercial safety & security window film in Christchurch for the risks you have identified and any written requirements. The pane, frame and fitting are part of the proposed system.

Safety film for suitable glass

Discuss help holding broken pieces together. The selected film and fitting need to suit the pane and its use.

The frame matters too

Ask whether the film needs a fixing at the frame. Keep that detail with the proposed product and test information.

Check what is already there

Include known coatings, glass type and damage. Film does not remove every need to replace unsafe glass or carry out other work.

Anti-graffiti window films

For scratching or etching, look at a replaceable surface layer. It is not proof of safety or security performance.

Frosted and Decorative window films

Raise privacy as a separate need. A finish that hides the view does not prove how a pane behaves if hit.

Common Questions

Questions about Commercial safety & security window film

Does film make commercial glass unbreakable?+

No. Glass can still break. Suitable film can help hold pieces together, with the result depending on the glass, film, fitting and incident.

Safety & security options
Will it stop every break-in?+

No such guarantee should be assumed. Ask which test data supports the glass, frame, film and any fixings proposed for your building.

Safety & security options
Can you discuss a written building requirement?+

Yes. We discuss Commercial safety & security window film in Christchurch for the concerns you describe. The proposed product and work must be checked for that use.

Commercial options
Is this a replacement for anti-graffiti film?+

No. Anti-graffiti film protects a surface from suitable types of marking. Safety and security films have different jobs and need their own checks.

Anti-graffiti options
What should I send first?+

Send photos of the panes and frames, known glass details and the written need. Name who approves the work and who can provide access.
