Questions, answered

What to know
before you choose.

Will window film make my rooms too dark?+

Not every film has a dark finish. We help you compare Window film in Christchurch using suitable samples, looking at daylight, colour and reflection from both sides. A sample makes it easier to judge the change before you decide.

Residential options
Does privacy film work after dark?+

Reflective film relies on the light balance and does not provide reliable privacy with lights on inside at night. Frosted film obscures the view while admitting soft, scattered light. Opaque options vary in light transmission.

Privacy options
Can film be fitted to double glazing?+

Suitability depends on the glass and film, including coatings and exposure. Choosing Window tint in Christchurch starts with checking your glass and how the room is used. We explain suitable products rather than assume every pane can take the same film.

Residential options
How do I care for newly fitted film?+

Follow the drying time and cleaning guidance for the selected product. Avoid abrasive materials, and contact us about persistent bubbles, lifting edges or other concerns.

Get a Quote
How much does window film cost?+

The quote depends on the selected film, pane sizes, number of windows, glass condition and access. Send photographs and approximate measurements if you have them. We can explain what needs checking before a project-specific quote is agreed. You do not need to know the product name to start.

Get a Quote
Can you help with one room or a small job?+

Yes. Tell us about the window that needs attention, even if the rest of the property works well. We can discuss suitability, a finish that sits alongside adjoining glass and the practical next step. There is no need to plan a whole-property installation before asking for advice.
