The problem, and a better way to use the space

Which window problem is affecting your home?

A lounge that gets too hot

Use your favourite seat again. Heat-control film can reduce solar heat through suitable glass, helping make sunny living areas more comfortable without covering the window all day.

Screens lost in bright light

Watch television or work at your desk with less distracting brightness. Anti-glare film can soften the light while keeping a level of daylight that suits the room.

Carpets and furnishings fading

Sunlight can fade carpets, timber flooring, curtains, sofas and artwork. Fade-reduction film can reduce UV exposure and manage light or heat, depending on the product. It cannot stop every cause of fading.

Neighbours looking into your home

Enjoy more daytime privacy without keeping the curtains closed. Reflective film depends on the light balance; frosting is an option where privacy matters more than a clear view.

Bathrooms and entrances on show

Obscure the view through bathroom windows, front-door glass and sidelights. Frosted film admits soft light; opaque products vary, so compare a sample before deciding.

Glass you are worried about

Ask about film that can help hold broken glass together when the glass and installation are suitable. Safety film has a different job from privacy or surface-protection film.

Common Questions

Questions about residential window film

Do I need every window tinted?+

No. Start with the rooms that need attention. Different rooms may need different films, and some windows may not need treatment. We can help you compare a targeted approach with a larger installation.

Residential options
Can I keep the view and natural light?+

Many transparent films preserve a view out, with different levels of colour and reflection. Frosted and opaque products obscure the outlook. Compare a suitable sample inside and outside before making your choice.

Residential options
Does privacy film work at night?+

Reflective film is a daytime option when outside is brighter than inside. It does not give reliable privacy with lights on inside after dark. Frosted film obscures the view in both conditions; its coverage still needs planning.

Privacy options
Can film go on double glazing?+

The film must suit the glass, including its coatings and exposure. We check the application before recommending a product. Do not choose a film on colour or price alone.

Residential options
Can you help me choose a film for my house?+

Yes. For House window tinting in Christchurch, share the room, approximate pane sizes and your main concern. We can discuss samples, suitability, installation and care without expecting you to choose a product first.
