The people behind the work

Meet Bill and Roxana.

Bill started Tint A Window in 1987. After retraining from painting and decorating, he brought the same care for preparation and finish to window film.

Today, that experience helps you choose around your own windows, rather than a shade alone. Bill considers the glass, the light and the result you want before recommending a suitable film.

Roxana grew up around the business and now leads its day-to-day running. From your first enquiry to planning the work, she helps keep the details clear and the next steps straightforward.

From today back to the beginning

Our story, since 1987.

Four original photographs from the family albums show the early days of Tint A Window.

  1. 2026

    39 years of window film experience

    Today, Bill and Roxana help customers choose film for homes and workplaces, with practical advice and a personal point of contact.

  2. Mid to late 1990s

    Taking the work on the road

    The branded van became part of the working day. This original photograph comes from the family archive.

  3. Mid-1990s

    Letting people see the finish

    Film samples and installation photographs helped customers understand the options. Seeing a suitable sample is still a useful part of choosing today.

  4. 1992

    Meeting customers at the Ashburton show

    A tent, a display and examples of the work brought window film to a local audience.

  5. Late 1980s

    An early Ashburton display

    At Ashburton Boulevard Day, samples and photographs gave people a closer look at what window film could do.

  6. 1987

    Bill starts Tint A Window

    The beginning of a family business built around a practical trade and careful work.

How we work with you

Clear advice.
Careful work.

Whether you want a cooler living room, more privacy or less glare at work, we begin with how you use the space. We check the glass and discuss suitable films, including their appearance and any limits.

You can ask about samples, installation and care before making a decision. We explain the proposed work so you know what is included and who to contact if you need help afterwards.
