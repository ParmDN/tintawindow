Service and related-option images follow the supplied photo categories. General safety/security glass photographs show an application context, not proof of a particular protective film.

Shared changes tighten hero spacing, align advice sections, make gallery links more visible and organise footer locations into a compact group. The original logo layout uses a brighter yellow across the site. Longer card groups support phone scrolling, while reviews retain vertical movement and direct interaction. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

Tint-guide percentages are illustrative visible light transmission (VLT) examples. They explain the amount of visible light passing through, not UV rejection, heat reduction, energy savings or the products stocked. Exact film figures require the selected product's supporting data.
