Advice, installation & aftercare

Advice for your working space

Task-specific light control

A presentation screen, café table and classroom board use daylight differently. Start with what people need to see and do.

A whole-room perspective

Include lighting and screen placement in the assessment. The treated window may be only one source of distracting reflection.

Sample decisions with users

Ask the people using the space what bothers them. Their normal seat and working hours help make the comparison relevant.

How It Works

Choose light control for the people using the space

01

Show the user’s view

Photograph the screen, board or work surface from the affected seat. Note the time of day rather than judging glare from the doorway.

02

Check the room layout

Include other windows, lighting and reflections. Identify the sunlight entering through each proposed treated pane and the daylight needed for the task.

03

Compare samples at work

Consider monitors, customer seating or teaching areas as appropriate. The useful light level may differ between rooms in the same building.

04

Agree access around people

Confirm the product, panes and approvals. Discuss desk preparation, room availability and any areas that need to stay open during the work.

05

Explain care to the team

Keep cleaning guidance with the building records. Describe any later glare concern from the original user position to make follow-up advice more useful.

Common Questions

Questions about Commercial anti-glare window film

Can film remove every reflection?+

No. It can reduce sunlight through the treated glass, but lighting, other windows and screen position may still create reflections. Show us what the user sees so those factors can be considered.

Commercial options
Is this suitable for schools and classrooms?+

Commercial anti-glare window films in Christchurch can be considered for suitable educational glass. Tell us about boards, screens, seating and access, and we will discuss the appropriate assessment.

Commercial options
Will the workplace look too dark?+

Products offer different levels of light reduction. Compare a suitable sample in the space, considering the people who use it and the daylight they need for their work.

Commercial options
Can one film help with heat as well?+

Some films combine glare and solar heat control. Where cooling is used, less incoming heat may also reduce electricity demand. That depends on the selected film, the building and its use; a glare rating alone does not establish savings.

Heat & solar control options
Can you install while we are open?+

We discuss your operation, access and the work area before confirming arrangements. Practical scheduling is agreed for the site, with any disruption explained.
