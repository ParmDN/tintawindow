The people behind the work Meet Bill and Roxana. Bill started Tint A Window in 1987. After retraining from painting and decorating, he brought the same care for preparation and finish to window film. Today, that experience helps you choose around your own windows, rather than a shade alone. Bill considers the glass, the light and the result you want before recommending a suitable film. Roxana grew up around the business and now leads its day-to-day running. From your first enquiry to planning the work, she helps keep the details clear and the next steps straightforward.
