/window-tinting-faq | h2
Choosing window film

/window-tinting-faq | summary
What is window film? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Window film is a specialist film applied to suitable glass surfaces. Different films can be selected to help with heat, glare, fading, privacy, appearance, safety, security or anti-graffiti requirements.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Does window film make rooms dark? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Not necessarily. Films are available in different shades and specifications. The right option can be selected around your requirements for heat, glare, privacy, natural light and outlook.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Can I still see outside through window film? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Many films can preserve the outlook through your windows. Frosted and opaque films are different because they are primarily designed to provide privacy rather than clear views.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Does mirror film provide privacy at night? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Mirror film is mainly suited to daytime privacy when it is brighter outside than inside. It does not give reliable privacy with lights on inside after dark.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Can window film help reduce fading? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Yes. Window film can help reduce sunlight-related fading. Fading is influenced by UV, visible light and heat, so the appropriate film should be selected for the level of protection required.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Is window film better than blinds or curtains? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
It depends on what you want to achieve. Blinds and curtains can block light and views, while suitable window film can help manage heat, glare, UV and privacy while retaining natural light and the outlook.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
What information should I compare between films? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Compare the information that relates to your main concern. For a sunny room, that includes heat control as well as visible light and reflection. For privacy, consider the view from both sides and the light balance at the hours the room is used.

/window-tinting-faq | p
A sample helps with appearance, but a sample alone cannot establish compatibility with your glass. Ask for the proposed product and the reason it suits the glass. Coatings, pane size and the installation setting need to be considered together.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Can one film solve every concern? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Sometimes one suitable product can address several priorities, but every selection involves choices. A finish that improves daytime privacy may change reflection. Reducing more daylight may help glare but change the room on an overcast day. Frosted coverage obscures the outlook.

/window-tinting-faq | p
List the outcomes in order of importance and tell us what you want to preserve. We can discuss the trade-offs and whether different panes need different products. There is no need to choose a whole-property finish before assessing the rooms.

Film appearance can change while an installation dries. Follow the care guidance for the selected product and contact us about persistent bubbles, lifting edges or other concerns. The appropriate response depends on the film, its condition and the installation details.

Privacy in daylight and after dark

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Does privacy film allow natural light through? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Yes. Certain privacy films can provide privacy while still allowing natural light into the space. Frosted and opaque films provide a different level of privacy by obscuring the view.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Can privacy film provide privacy during the day and night? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
The level of privacy depends on the film. Reflective or mirror films are mainly suited to daytime privacy. Frosted and opaque films can provide privacy without relying on the difference in light levels.

Commercial

/window-tinting-faq | h2
Film for workplaces

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Can window film help reduce glare in offices? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Yes. Anti-glare window film can be selected to help make bright work areas and computer screens more comfortable.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Can window film help with excessive heat in commercial buildings? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Heat rejection and solar protection films can be selected to help manage solar heat entering suitable commercial spaces.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Can you install anti-graffiti film? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Yes. Anti-graffiti film is designed for suitable glass applications where scratching, tagging or surface damage is a concern.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Can window film be used on glass partitions? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
Yes. Frosted, opaque and decorative films can be considered for suitable internal glass partitions where privacy or appearance is required.

/window-tinting-faq | summary
Are anti-graffiti and safety films the same thing? +

/window-tinting-faq | p
No. Anti-graffiti film is a replaceable protective layer intended for suitable glass where certain scratching or tagging damage is a concern. Damaged film may be replaced as part of maintenance. It does not repair damage already present in the underlying glass.

/window-tinting-faq | p
Safety and security films are specified for different protective purposes, with the glass, film, frames and installation affecting performance. Discuss the actual concern and request evidence for the proposed system. Do not assume that a film chosen for privacy or solar control provides the same protection.

Include the property location, the room or building use, your main concern and the approximate number of panes. Photographs and rough dimensions can help the first conversation, but you do not need to reach difficult glass to obtain them.

/window-tinting-faq | p
For shared or commercial buildings, identify the decision-maker, any permissions required and access restrictions. A clear plan for the work helps separate the proposed product and installation from assumptions about timing, appearance or performance.
