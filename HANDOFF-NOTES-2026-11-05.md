## SEO discussion with Ashut, 2 November

Ashut wants to retain the homepage's Christchurch targeting and search title
while showing wider coverage. His suggested small Christchurch H1 plus dominant
New Zealand headline is a proposal, not implemented in this package.

The current recommendation discussed with Sagar is:

- Home retains window tinting Christchurch as its main search target, with
  window tint Christchurch / window film Christchurch as related terms.
- Residential owns residential/house window tinting Christchurch; Commercial
  owns commercial window tinting Christchurch.
- Each individual service owns its specific service and audience, retaining
  local relevance naturally; town pages target their actual town.
- Clear customer-facing headings and an immediate South Island coverage line
  can reduce repetition without abandoning all local relevance.
- Do not make the Christchurch location page a second generic version of Home.
- Verify NZ-country ranking data, winning URLs and dates before reallocating
  established queries. The supplied cropped ranking screenshot appears to use
  Australian flags and does not show winning URLs/dates. Its quoted positions
  have not been independently confirmed as current NZ/homepage positions.
- Do not treat three literal repetitions or a fixed word count as a ranking
  guarantee. Existing checks record the previous brief and grammatical variants;
  align them with the final agreed editorial brief when the copy is changed.

Sagar requested suggestions first. These are proposed decisions, not a record
that he has approved changing the full keyword strategy. Coordinate with him
and Ashut while completing the final client updates.
